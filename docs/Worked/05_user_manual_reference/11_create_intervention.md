# Create Intervention

Data Ops & Enterprise ➔ Risk & Interventions Engine

## 01. Create Intervention Sub-Manual

Operational instructions for proposing manual HR follow-up actions and retention plans.

### Proposing a New Intervention Step-by-Step:

- **Select Target Scope:** Choose whether the action applies to an individual ( `EMPLOYEE` ), a team ( `TEAM` ), an entire department ( `DEPARTMENT` ), or organization-wide ( `ORGANIZATION` ).
- **Assign HRBP Owner & Priority:** Assign the accountable HR Business Partner owner and select urgency level ( `LOW` , `MEDIUM` , `HIGH` , or `CRITICAL` ).
- **Formulate Strategy & Budget:** Allocate planned retention budget ($) and document expected outcome metrics (e.g. *"1-on-1 workload rebalancing and compensation review"* ).
- **Save Intervention Ticket:** Clicking Save creates a planned ticket record ( `status: planned` ). Aurelinx does not automatically discipline, contact, or modify employee source records.
