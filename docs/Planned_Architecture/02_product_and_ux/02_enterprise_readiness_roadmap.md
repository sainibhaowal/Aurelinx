# 02. Enterprise Readiness Roadmap

01. **Live Connector Credentials & Sync Verification:**
    - Connect real sandbox/production instances of Workday, Greenhouse, Slack, and Jira using tenant-scoped OAuth/API credentials stored in a secret manager.

02. **Closed-Loop Interventions:**
    - Connect Risk & Sentiment signals to structured retention interventions (`interventions`, `intervention_outcomes`) with 30/60/90-day follow-up tracking.

03. **Regional Compliance & Audit Automation:**
    - Expand EU AI Act / GDPR policy packs (`compliance_policies`), automated retention enforcement (`retention_policies`), and signed procurement/DR evidence exports.
